# Interface

Mode: Operate. The user's accepted concept is a compact mobile queue with administration on the same screen. A class register gives the interface its structure: numbered rows, a readable subject title, clear attendance states, and a distinct personal row. White and cool light gray surfaces are appropriate for bright classrooms; deep blue is the action color, orange denotes the participant's place. No stock illustrations or invented queue entries.

Manrope is bundled locally with Cyrillic. Body text is at least 16px, labels 14px. Rows use tabular numerals. A desktop side panel contains identity and session controls; on phones these precede the queue. The active queue and past classes are two tabs. Forms are inline; native modal dialogs are reserved for protected admin login and destructive confirmation. Controls have visible keyboard focus, comfortable touch targets and reduced-motion handling.

Loading, unconfigured deployment, connection loss, no active class, empty queue, closed registration, waiting, completed and archived states are explicit. Offline screens do not claim the displayed queue is live.

Responsive refinement: one column up to 900 CSS px, two columns above that. At widths up to 480 px, administrator row actions follow the participant name on their own line. All action targets are at least 44×44 px. Narrow add-person forms wrap instead of squeezing the name input. Dialogs scroll on short screens; safe-area insets are respected.
